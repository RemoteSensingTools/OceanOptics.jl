Pure (sea)water

OceanOptics.PureWater — Type
struct PureWater{FT, A<:AbstractPureWaterAbsorptionModel, S<:AbstractPureWaterScatteringModel} <: AbstractAbsorbingScatterer{FT}

Pure (sea)water as an optical constituent.

Fields

  • absorption_model::AbstractPureWaterAbsorptionModel: Absorption-model choice (tag)

  • scattering_model::AbstractPureWaterScatteringModel: Scattering-model choice (tag)

  • temperature::Any: Temperature [°C]

  • salinity::Any: Salinity [PSU] (Practical Salinity Unit)

  • depolarization::Any: Depolarization ratio δ (unpolarized light, 90°) of the phase matrix and of the ZhangHu2009 scattering magnitude

Examples

# Default: IOCCG2018 absorption, Zhang-Hu scattering, S = 35 PSU, T = 20 °C

# Legacy Pope-Fry + Morel combination
water = PureWater{Float64}(

    scattering_model = Morel1974(),
)

# Cold, freshwater Arctic case
water = PureWater{Float64}(temperature = 2.0, salinity = 2.0)

OceanOptics.IOCCG2018 — Type
struct IOCCG2018 <: AbstractPureWaterAbsorptionModel

Pure-water absorption recommended by the IOCCG Protocol Series (2018); the default model.

It combines Morel et al. (2007) for 340–415 nm, Pope & Fry (1997) for 420–725 nm and Kou et al. (1993) for 730–750 nm, at 5 nm over 350–750 nm as used for the HydroLight database of Loisel et al. (2023), and is continued with Kou et al. (1993) up to 2500 nm. The composite's 730–750 nm values differ from the bundled Kou table by up to 0.8 % (0.06 % step at the 750 nm junction).

OceanOptics.Morel1974 — Type
struct Morel1974 <: AbstractPureWaterScatteringModel

Power law fitted to the seawater scattering of Morel (1974),

$b_w(λ) = 0.00193\,(550/λ)^{4.32}$ [1/m], λ in nm.

The formula represents seawater and does not depend on the salinity, temperature or depolarization of the PureWater: with salinity = 0 it still returns the seawater value, about 30 % above fresh water (compare ZhangHu2009, 1.958·10⁻³ m⁻¹ at 500 nm for S = 0 against 2.913·10⁻³ here). It is a smooth fit, not Morel's table: against the pure-sea-water values that Mobley et al. (1993, Problem 7) quote from Smith & Baker (1981), 0.0063 m⁻¹ at 417 nm and 0.0032 m⁻¹ at 486 nm, it gives 0.00638 (+1.3 %) and 0.00329 (+2.9 %); the printed values carry two digits. It is 7–16 % above ZhangHu2009 at S = 35 over 350–750 nm.

source
OceanOptics.ZhangHu2009 — Type
struct ZhangHu2009 <: AbstractPureWaterScatteringModel

Scattering of seawater after Zhang, Hu & He (2009), a function of wavelength, temperature and salinity.

The Rayleigh magnitude uses the depolarization of the PureWater (default 0.039) in the Cabannes factor (6 + 6δ)/(6 − 7δ) and in the angular integral.

No range of validity is checked: the routine returns values at any wavelength, temperature and salinity. The refractive index inside it is the Quan & Fry (1995) fit (400–700 nm, 0–30 °C, 0–35 PSU); outside those ranges the result is an extrapolation.

OceanOptics.QuanFry1995 — Type
struct QuanFry1995{FT<:Real} <: AbstractRefractiveIndexModel

Quan & Fry (1995) empirical refractive index of seawater relative to air,

n = n₀ + (n₁ + n₂T + n₃T²) S + n₄T² + (n₅ + n₆S + n₇T)/λ + n₈/λ² + n₉/λ³

  • salinity::Real: Salinity [PSU]

Phytoplankton

OceanOptics.Phytoplankton — Type
struct Phytoplankton{FT, M<:AbstractPhytoplanktonModel, P<:AbstractOceanPhaseFunction{FT}, G<:Union{Nothing, AbstractPigmentPartition}, S<:AbstractParticleScatteringModel} <: AbstractAbsorbingScatterer{FT}

Particles of Case-1 water (phytoplankton and co-varying detritus) as an absorbing scatterer, parameterized by the chlorophyll-a concentration.

Chl is the chlorophyll-a concentration [mg/m³]. The model tag M selects the absorption parameterization; with Bricaud1998 it is the particulate absorption $a_p = A(λ) Chl^{E(λ)}$, which includes detritus (see there before combining with NonAlgalParticles). Scattering is the total particulate scattering of Case-1 water given by scattering_model, by default GordonMorel1983, $b_p = 0.30 Chl^{0.62} (550/λ)$, and the phase function defaults to FournierForandPhase with backscatter fraction B = 0.01.

An optional pigment partition (ProspectPigments, Bidigare1990Pigments) attributes the absorption to pigment groups: absorption_parts then names the groups, layer_optics reports one absorption component per group, and chlorophyll_fluorescence can weight each group by its energy transfer to chlorophyll. The total absorption is unchanged.

Fields

  • model::AbstractPhytoplanktonModel: Parameterization-model tag (e.g. Bricaud1998())

  • Chl::Any: Chlorophyll-a concentration [mg/m³]

  • phase::AbstractOceanPhaseFunction{FT} where FT: Particulate-scattering phase function

  • pigments::Union{Nothing, AbstractPigmentPartition}: Pigment partition of the absorption, or nothing (a single part, :total)

  • scattering_model::AbstractParticleScatteringModel: Scattering law and its parameters (default GordonMorel1983)

Examples

# Open-ocean Case-1 water at Chl = 0.5 mg/m³
phyto = Phytoplankton{Float64, Bricaud1998}(Chl = 0.5)

# Custom backscatter fraction (coastal, larger cells)
phyto = Phytoplankton{Float64, Bricaud1998}(Chl = 2.0, B = 0.015)

# Absorption attributed to chlorophyll and carotenoids
phyto = Phytoplankton(Chl = 0.5, pigments = ProspectPigments(carotenoid_ratio = 0.3))
absorption_parts(phyto)           # (:chlorophyll, :carotenoids, :unassigned)

phyto = Phytoplankton(Chl = 0.5, scattering_model = GordonMorel1983(b_ref = 0.45))

OceanOptics.Bricaud1998 — Type
struct Bricaud1998 <: AbstractPhytoplanktonModel

Particulate absorption of Case-1 water after Bricaud et al. (1998, JGR 103, 31033).

The absorption is $a_p(λ) = A(λ) Chl^{E(λ)}$ in 1/m with Chl in mg/m³ (A(440) = 0.0520, E(440) = 0.6350). It is the absorption of all particles, phytoplankton plus the detritus that co-varies with it, not of phytoplankton alone: adding a separate NonAlgalParticles constituent counts detrital absorption twice, unless a detrital or mineral load beyond the Case-1 background is intended.

A(λ) and E(λ) are the tables of HydroLight's Case-1 model (AEmidUVabs.txt), bundled as data/bricaud_1998_A.csv and data/bricaud_1998_E.csv: 300–1000 nm at 2 nm, with the UV part after Morrison & Nelson (2004) and Vasilkov et al. (2005). Chl = 0 gives zero absorption.

OceanOptics.GordonMorel1983 — Type
struct GordonMorel1983{FT<:Real} <: AbstractParticleScatteringModel

Total particulate scattering of Case-1 water after Gordon & Morel (1983), as cited by Fell (1997), Eq. 5.5:

$b_p(λ) = b_\mathrm{ref}\, Chl^{m}\, (λ_\mathrm{ref}/λ)^{γ}$ [1/m], Chl in mg/m³,

with b_ref = 0.30 m⁻¹ at λ_ref = 550 nm, m = 0.62 and γ = 1. Fell gives b_ref = 0.30 ± 0.15. It is the scattering of all particles that co-vary with chlorophyll, not of living cells alone; in coastal or turbid water the particulate scattering can be larger by a factor of 2 or more.

Fields

  • b_ref::Real: Scattering coefficient at λ_ref for Chl = 1 mg/m³ [1/m]

  • chl_exponent::Real: Exponent m of the chlorophyll concentration

  • λ_ref::Real: Reference wavelength [nm]

  • spectral_exponent::Real: Exponent γ of the spectral law (λ_ref/λ)^γ

OceanOptics.Bidigare1990Pigments — Type
struct Bidigare1990Pigments{FT<:Real, X<:AbstractOutOfRangeShares} <: AbstractPigmentPartition

Partition into the algal pigment groups of Bidigare et al. (1990), with their in vivo weight-specific absorption spectra (data/bidigare_1990_pigments.csv, 400–750 nm, from the Ocean Optics Web Book). Fields are pigment concentrations [mg m⁻³], e.g. from HPLC; only their ratios matter, since the total comes from the phytoplankton model.

Parts: (:chlorophyll_a, :chlorophyll_b, :chlorophyll_c, :photosynthetic_carotenoids, :photoprotective_carotenoids, :phycoerythrin_peb, :phycoerythrin_pub, :unassigned). Fields

  • chl_b::Real: Chlorophyll b [mg m⁻³]

  • chl_c::Real: Chlorophyll c [mg m⁻³]

  • psc::Real: Photosynthetic carotenoids (e.g. fucoxanthin, peridinin) [mg m⁻³]

  • ppc::Real: Photoprotective carotenoids (e.g. zeaxanthin, diadinoxanthin, β-carotene) [mg m⁻³]

  • pe_peb::Real: Phycoerythrin rich in phycoerythrobilin (Synechococcus WH7803 type) [mg m⁻³]

  • pe_pub::Real: Phycoerythrin rich in phycourobilin (Synechococcus WH8103 type) [mg m⁻³]

  • outside::AbstractOutOfRangeShares: Rule for the shares outside the tabulated 400–750 nm

Example

OceanOptics.TabulatedAbsorber — Type
struct TabulatedAbsorber{FT, L<:AbstractTableLookup} <: AbstractAbsorber{FT}

Absorber defined by tabulated absorption coefficients at exact wavelengths.

Fields

  • λ::Vector: Wavelengths [nm], strictly increasing

  • a::Vector: Absorption coefficient [1/m] at each λ

  • total::Bool: Whether a is the bulk absorption of the whole layer

  • source::String: Provenance: citation, table, and any transformation applied

  • lookup::AbstractTableLookup: ExactLookup() (tabulated wavelengths only) or LinearLookup() (interpolate between them)

Construction

TabulatedAbsorber(λ, a; total = false, source = "", lookup = :exact)

λ [nm] must be strictly increasing; a [1/m] finite and non-negative. Set total = true when a is the bulk absorption of the whole layer (all absorbers, including water): layer_optics then requires every other constituent in the layer to have zero absorption.

A query wavelength matches a tabulated one when they agree to a relative 1e-6, which absorbs the round-off of Float32 wavelengths and of grids computed from wavenumbers as 1e7/ν. With lookup = :exact any other wavelength throws; with lookup = :linear the coefficient is interpolated linearly between the neighbouring tabulated wavelengths (a DomainError outside the table). lookup also accepts an ExactLookup or LinearLookup; the choice is the type parameter L. Exact lookups return the table's precision; interpolated values have the type that the query and the table promote to, so a ForwardDiff.Dual wavelength gives the slope of the segment.

Example

# Chowdhary et al. (2020) Table 2, AOS-II/III: a_blk = a_w
a_blk = TabulatedAbsorber([350.0, 450.0, 550.0, 650.0],
                          [0.0204, 0.0092, 0.0565, 0.3400];
                          total = true, source = "Chowdhary et al. 2020, Table 2")
absorption(a_blk, 450.0)   # 0.0092
absorption(a_blk, 500.0)   # ArgumentError: not a tabulated wavelength

OceanOptics.TabulatedScatterer — Type
struct TabulatedScatterer{FT, P<:AbstractOceanPhaseFunction{FT}, L<:AbstractTableLookup} <: AbstractScatterer{FT}

Non-absorbing scatterer defined by tabulated scattering coefficients at exact wavelengths and a phase function (or phase matrix).

Fields

  • λ::Vector: Wavelengths [nm], strictly increasing

  • b::Vector: Scattering coefficient [1/m] at each λ

  • phase::AbstractOceanPhaseFunction{FT} where FT: Phase function or phase matrix

  • source::String: Provenance: citation, table, and any transformation applied

  • lookup::AbstractTableLookup: ExactLookup() (tabulated wavelengths only) or LinearLookup() (interpolate between them)

Construction

TabulatedScatterer(λ, b, phase; source = "", lookup = :exact)

λ [nm] must be strictly increasing; b [1/m] finite and non-negative; wavelengths are matched, and with lookup = :linear interpolated, as for TabulatedAbsorber (the phase function keeps its own wavelength lookup). phase is any AbstractOceanPhaseFunction; a wavelength-dependent TabulatedGreekPhase must tabulate every wavelength in λ. The backscattering coefficient is derived from the phase function, b_b = b · B, so the two can never disagree; for a TabulatedGreekPhase, B is that of the tabulated (possibly truncated) expansion. Model an absorbing component as a TabulatedAbsorber plus a TabulatedScatterer.

Example

# Chowdhary et al. (2020) AOS-II/III: pure-seawater scattering, δ_w = 0
water_scat = TabulatedScatterer([350.0, 450.0, 550.0, 650.0],
                                [0.0134, 0.0045, 0.0019, 0.0010],
                                RayleighWaterPhase(depolarization = 0.0);
                                source = "Chowdhary et al. 2020, Table 2")
