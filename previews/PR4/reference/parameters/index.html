Physical parameters

Every physical parameter of the package is a field of a model type or a keyword argument with a default. This page lists them per model with the default, the unit, where the number comes from, and how to set it. Coefficient sets that define a published model as a whole (the Quan–Fry polynomial, the Zhang–Hu–He formulas, the tabulated data files) are named constants or data files; the choice between such models is made by type.

"Package default" marks a value that is a modelling choice of this package and not taken from a paper that was checked for this release.

Pure water: PureWater

parametersymboldefaultunitsourceset with
temperatureT20°Cpackage defaultPureWater(temperature = 5)
salinityS35PSUpackage defaultPureWater(salinity = 0)
depolarization ratio (unpolarized light, 90°)δ0.039–Farinato & Rowell (1976), as used by Zhang, Hu & He (2009)PureWater(depolarization = 0.09)
absorption table–IOCCG2018–see the model typesPureWater(absorption_model = PopeFry1997())
scattering model–ZhangHu2009–see the model typesPureWater(scattering_model = Morel1974())

δ is one stored value: it sets the phase matrix (RayleighWaterPhase from scattering_phase) and the magnitude of the ZhangHu2009 scattering, b ∝ (6 + 6δ)/(6 − 7δ) · (2 + δ)/(1 + δ). Morel1974 scattering is an empirical seawater law and uses neither δ, T nor S. Temperature enters the absorption through the coefficient Ψ_T of Röttgers et al. (2014), applied from 550 nm upward relative to the 22 °C of the Pope & Fry and Kou tables; salinity does not enter the absorption.

Fixed coefficient sets: Morel1974 (0.00193 m⁻¹ at 550 nm, exponent 4.32); the Zhang, Hu & He (2009) formulas with their auxiliary fits; the absorption tables in data/.
